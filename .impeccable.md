## Design Context

### Users
Self-hosters and builders who run Octop locally/LAN and need a phone companion to pick experts, manage threads, and chat on the go.

### Brand Personality
Warm · Precise · Companionable  
Emotional goal: calm confidence while talking to agents — not flashy “AI product,” not enterprise gray.

### Aesthetic Direction
Octop **Elegant Rose** (`#E85D75`) on soft light surfaces (`#F5F6F8` layout, white cards, blush assistant bubbles `#FFF7F9`). Soft Nordic restraint adapted to Octop tokens: 16px cards, 12px buttons, 36px pill tab bar, asymmetric chat bubbles. Logo from official Octop assets. Anti-references: purple AI glow, dense dashboards, flat unthemed Expo defaults.

### Design Principles
1. Brand first on login; rose accent reserved for primary actions and active states.
2. One job per screen; hierarchy via type size + rose fill, not decoration.
3. Chat is the product surface — readable markdown, clear streaming/working states.
4. Touch-first: 44–52px controls, floating pill tabs with solid active fill.
5. Stay faithful to Octop web/desktop tokens rather than inventing a new palette.
